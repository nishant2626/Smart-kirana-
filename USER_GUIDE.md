# Smart-Kirana User Guide

Welcome to the **Smart-Kirana** user guide. This application is designed to help local Indian shopkeepers manage their inventory, sales, and business metrics with a modern, simple interface.

## 1. Installation
1. Obtain the **Smart-Kirana APK** file.
2. Open the APK on your Android device.
3. If prompted, allow "Installation from unknown sources" in your settings.
4. Follow the on-screen instructions to complete the installation.

## 2. Getting Started
### Login
1. Launch the app from your home screen.
2. Enter your **Email Address** and **Password** (min 6 characters).
3. Click **Login** to enter your dashboard.
4. *Presentation Bypass:* If you are testing offline, use `admin@store.com` and `admin123`.

### Creating an Account
1. On the Login screen, click **"New User? Create Account"**.
2. Enter your email and a secure password.
3. Click the registration button. Once successful, you will be redirected to the dashboard.

## 3. Using the Dashboard
The Dashboard is your command center. It shows:
- **Total Products**: The count of all items in your store.
- **Low Stock Alerts**: Items that need immediate restocking (Stock ≤ 5).
- **Today's Sales**: A summary of your daily performance.
- **Quick Actions**: Rapid access to common tasks.

## 4. Managing Inventory
1. From the dashboard, click **"Manage Inventory"**.
2. Use the **Search bar** at the top to find specific products by name.
3. Look for **Red status text** (e.g., "Stock: 3") to identify items running low.
4. Click the **Floating Action Button (+)** at the bottom right to add a new product.

## 5. Adding a Product
1. Open the **Add New Product** screen.
2. Enter the **Product Name**, **Price**, **Stock Quantity**, and **Category**.
3. Click **Save Product**.
4. The app will sync this data with the cloud instantly.

## 6. Understanding Notifications
- The app automatically monitors your stock levels.
- If an item's stock falls to **5 units or less**, you will receive a standard Android **system notification**.
- Tap the notification to open the app and check your inventory.

## 7. Troubleshooting
- **"Auth Failed"**: Ensure your internet is on and your password is at least 6 characters.
- **"Network Error"**: Check your Wi-Fi or mobile data. Click **Retry** on the inventory screen to reload data.
- **"Permission Denied"**: If using Android 13+, ensure you allow notification permissions when prompted.

## 8. Logging Out
1. On the Dashboard, click the **Power icon** in the top right corner.
2. You will be securely logged out and returned to the login screen.
